# Privacy Policy - Go Silent by MPS

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026

Go Silent by MPS ("the app") is developed by MPS. This policy explains what the
app does and does not do with your information.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

The app has no account system, no login, and no server of its own. Everything
you set in the app - your timer duration and your choice of Silent or Do Not
Disturb mode - stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Do Not Disturb access / notification policy access** - used only to switch
  your phone to Silent or Do Not Disturb for the duration you choose, and to
  restore your previous setting when the timer ends.
- **Notifications** - used only to show the ongoing timer notification while a
  silence session is running, so you can see the remaining time and stop it.
- **Foreground service** - keeps the timer running reliably so your sound is
  restored even if you leave the app.

None of these permissions are used to read your messages, calls, contacts,
location, or any other personal data.

## Advertising

The app displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google may collect
and use data to serve and measure ads, including device identifiers such as the
Advertising ID. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is
governed by Google's own policies:

- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your
consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at
any time from within the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any
information from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with
