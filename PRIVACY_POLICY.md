# Privacy Policy — Go Silent by MPS

_Effective date: [fill in before publishing]_

Go Silent by MPS ("the app") is published by MPS. This document explains what data the app collects, what it does with it, and your rights. This same text is linked from the app's own About screen.

## Data we collect ourselves

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no backend of our own. Your timer settings and history stay on your device only.

## Data collected by third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

Go Silent by MPS displays banner ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect:

- Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable identifier assigned by Android
- IP address
- Device information (model, OS version, language, coarse country)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Interaction data (whether an ad was seen or clicked)

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Consent (EEA, UK, and other regions where required)

On first launch, the app checks whether you're in a region where consent law applies (EEA, UK, and others depending on your AdMob account configuration). If it does, Google's consent form appears before any ad loads, letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change this choice later from the app's About screen (Privacy Policy link) or by reinstalling the app to see the form again.

You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

## Permissions and why we ask

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE | Load banner ads via AdMob |
| POST_NOTIFICATIONS | Show the ongoing timer notification |
| ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY | Switch DND on and off automatically |
| FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE | Keep the timer running when the app is minimized |
| WAKE_LOCK | Keep the timer ticking while device is idle |
| VIBRATE | Optional haptics |

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. If that changes, we will comply with Google Play's Families Policy and disable personalized advertising.

## Your choices

- Revoke any permission from Android Settings → Apps → Go Silent by MPS → Permissions
- Reset or opt out of Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads
- Contact us to ask about any data associated with your device
